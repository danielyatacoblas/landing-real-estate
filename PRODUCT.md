# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js 16 + React 19 + TypeScript, MapLibre GL 5 with OpenFreeMap (no API key), GSAP + Lenis, static prerender on Vercel, node:test for the commute model.

## Users

Primary: recruiters and clients reviewing Daniel Yataco's front-end work. In-fiction: Lima professionals buying their first apartment who lose hours a day in traffic.

## Product Purpose

Cerca is a fictional Lima real-estate agency. The page filters apartments by estimated commute time to the visitor's workplace and books visits.

## Positioning

The only listing page in Lima that starts with "where do you work and how many minutes do you accept", not with the district.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Real photography from Pexels. Listings, prices and approximate locations are demo data; commute times are labeled estimates.
